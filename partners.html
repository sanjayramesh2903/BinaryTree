🌐 Meet Our Partners

At BinaryTree, we’re proud to collaborate with grassroots organizations across Africa that share our vision of equitable digital education. These local partners help us bring technology and opportunity to underserved communities.

🇹🇿 TEDI Tanzania

TEDI (Transforming Education for Development Initiative) operates across rural Tanzania to bring innovation into education. With their support, we’ve run digital literacy workshops, provided refurbished laptops to schools, and helped train over 150 students in foundational computing.

🇰🇪 COHECF Kenya

COHECF (Community Health and Education Change Foundation) is a youth-led initiative in western Kenya. Their strong ties to local schools allow us to deliver beginner-friendly programming lessons in Scratch and Python, even in areas with limited internet access.

🇸🇳 GNO FAR Senegal

GNO FAR (Groupement National des Organisations Féminines pour l’Alphabétisation Rurale) focuses on education for girls and women in rural Senegal. Together, we’ve led bilingual workshops on graphic design, digital tools, and AI literacy in both French and Wolof.
